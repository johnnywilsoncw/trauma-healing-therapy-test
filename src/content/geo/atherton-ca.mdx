## Welcome!

At Trauma Healing Therapy (THT), we provide a warm and supportive environment where you can begin the process of healing from trauma and rebuilding meaningful connections. Our team is deeply committed to helping you overcome past challenges and foster a fulfilling and joyful life.

We understand that trauma can take many forms, whether it’s from a specific event or long-standing relational wounds like childhood trauma, divorce, loss, or infidelity. No matter what you’re facing, we’re here to offer guidance, understanding, and unwavering support every step of the way.

Don’t hesitate to reach out for a complimentary consultation. We are eager to listen to your story, understand your unique circumstances, and explore how we can best support you in moving forward.

With options for both virtual and in-person sessions, we proudly serve the diverse communities of Atherton, CA, welcoming anyone who seeks our help. It is our privilege to be a part of your journey toward healing and growth, and we are here to walk this path with you.
