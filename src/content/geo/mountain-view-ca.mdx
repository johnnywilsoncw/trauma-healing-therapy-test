## Welcome!

Welcome to Trauma Healing Therapy (THT), a compassionate space dedicated to helping you heal from trauma and strengthen your relationships. At THT, we are committed to supporting you as you move beyond past traumas and work towards a brighter, more fulfilling future.

Whether you’re dealing with the effects of a specific traumatic experience or want to address past relational wounds like childhood trauma, divorce, loss, or infidelity, we’re here to guide you every step of the way on your healing journey.

Reach out to us using the form for a free consultation. We’re here to discuss your unique situation and find out how we can best support you.

Offering both online and in-person sessions, we are proud to serve the diverse communities of Mountain View, CA and welcome anyone seeking support. It is our privilege to accompany you on your path to healing.
