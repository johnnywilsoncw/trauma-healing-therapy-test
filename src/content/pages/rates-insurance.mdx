## Rates

At Trauma Healing Therapy, we understand that starting therapy is a meaningful investment—of time, trust, and finances. That’s why we work collaboratively with you toward clearly defined goals, helping you move forward without unnecessary delays. Therapy with us doesn’t have to be an open-ended commitment—it’s about getting you the support you need, as efficiently and effectively as possible.

We are honest and transparent about the process and cost, and we will always prioritize what’s best for your well-being. Our rates reflect the average for high-quality therapeutic services in the dynamic San Francisco Bay Area and Cupertino. Every individual’s needs are unique, and we’d love to connect through [a free, confidential 20-minute consultation](https://traumahealingtherapy.janeapp.com/#/discipline/2/treatment/11). In this conversation, we can talk about what you’re looking for, explore what treatment might look like, and go over any questions you have about our fees or approach.

Trauma Healing Therapy is an out-of-network (OON) provider, which means clients are responsible for payment at the time of service. However, we are insurance-friendly, and many clients are able to receive reimbursement through their out-of-network benefits. You can easily check your coverage here: [mentaya.co/b/o0REePusDeDgQe41bfKn](https://mentaya.co/b/o0REePusDeDgQe41bfKn)

## Private pay/Out of Network OON

THT is an Out Of Network (OON) Provider, but we are an insurance friendly practice, since we have partnered with a service that helps check your out-of-network (OON) benefits and provides an estimate of what your insurance may reimburse for therapy. However, they do not guarantee reimbursement, and you are still the sole responsible for payment to our practice and claim submission.

We always recommend that you call your insurance provider directly to confirm the details of your coverage and benefits, but this is a great tool to start with having an idea of your OON benefits! Check it out yourself in the Mentaya box here!
