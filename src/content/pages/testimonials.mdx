## Dr. Mariella Lauriola

[![Psychology Today verified profile](../../assets/wp/2024/05/Psychology-Today-Verified-1.png)](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/therapists/trauma-healing-therapy-cupertino-ca/778701)

<Quote featured>

“I met Mariella in 2019 during an intro couples therapy session. Within ten minutes, she made me feel more seen than I had ever been in my entire life! She doesn’t just practice psychotherapy, she is a dedicated care provider. This is proven in her commitment to finding where she can be of assistance during each session, her above and beyond methods of sending beneficial resources outside of sessions, her gentle check-ins if she hasn’t heard from you, and her devotion to learning new methods of treatment.

In 2023 I transitioned from working with Mariella in couple’s therapy, to working with her in trauma therapy/recovery. My husband and I still use many of her teachings to connect with each other. Couples therapy or individual therapy, Mariella never makes you feel that you are alone. She reminds you of your humanity in the right moments, and to be forgiving of yourself to avoid self-inflicted trauma. She remains an instrumental part of my mental health and self-care journey today, and the sole person that “gets me”. 

I am truly thankful for Mariella as a person, and as a therapist. In another life, we would be best friends – her fiery personality and love for laughter are traits we share, and I imagine us having great fun together. I respect and cherish her. Her dedication to my progress encourages me to love myself more and motivates me to strive to be a better person. She is irreplaceable and a true gem.”

</Quote>
