<Faq question="How long does it take to feel better?">

This is one of the most common—and most human—questions we hear, and the truth is: it’s different for everyone. Some people begin to feel a sense of relief or hope after the very first session, simply by being heard and supported. For others, especially those carrying deep or long-standing trauma, it can take more time to begin noticing meaningful shifts.

Healing is not linear, and it’s okay if it takes time. What matters most is that you’re not doing it alone. At Trauma Healing Therapy, we’re here to walk alongside you with patience, compassion, and the tools you need to move forward. We establish treatment goals together (as long or short as you want, based on your unique needs). Our biggest goal is always the same: to help you feel safe, supported, and empowered to heal at your own pace. There is hope—even if you can’t see it yet, we see it for you. And in time, you’ll begin to feel it too.

</Faq>
