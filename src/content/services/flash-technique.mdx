The Flash Technique is a recently developed, minimally intrusive option to process traumatic memories without feeling distress, as clients are not asked to consciously engage with the traumatic memory. Flash was originally developed as an addition to the preparation phase of EMDR, but has recently demonstrated to be effective in reducing the disturbance level associated with severely painful memories, sometimes in as little as 10 minutes.
