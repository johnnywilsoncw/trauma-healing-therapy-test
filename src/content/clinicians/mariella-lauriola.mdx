## About Dr. Mariella

### From surviving to living — building a clinic where trauma healing is deeply human, clinically grounded, and relationally safe

Dr. Mariella Lauriola is the Founder, CEO, and Clinical Director of Trauma Healing Therapy, a private-pay group psychotherapy practice specializing in trauma therapy, EMDR therapy, couples therapy, relationship trauma, and intensive trauma treatment.

While Dr. Mariella is no longer accepting new therapy clients, her clinical heart remains at the center of the practice. Today, her work focuses on training and mentoring the trauma therapists, EMDR clinicians, and couples therapists at Trauma Healing Therapy, ensuring that each client receives thoughtful, deeply attuned, evidence-based care.

Dr. Mariella founded Trauma Healing Therapy with a clear mission: to create a place where people who have endured trauma do not feel pathologized, rushed, or alone — but instead feel safe, understood, and supported by clinicians who know how to hold both the science and the humanity of healing.

Before founding Trauma Healing Therapy, Dr. Mariella led a supportive housing program for women healing from trauma and psychiatric challenges. That experience shaped her belief that healing does not happen through perfection, performance, or clinical distance. Healing begins in safety, connection, dignity, and being truly seen. Sometimes that happened in formal therapy spaces; other times, it happened while rolling out pizza dough, sharing a meal, and talking honestly about life.

Over the past 15+ years, Dr. Mariella has worked across neuropsychological research, community mental health, intensive outpatient treatment, and private practice. Her background in neuropsychology and clinical neuroscience deepened her understanding of how trauma affects the brain and body, while her advanced training in EMDR, Emotionally Focused Therapy, and the Gottman Method continues to inform the clinical philosophy of the practice.

Today, Dr. Mariella’s role is to support the therapists who support the clients — through clinical consultation, EMDR training, couples therapy consultation, program development, community outreach, and ongoing mentorship.

## Dr. Mariella’s Role at Trauma Healing Therapy

As Founder and Clinical Director, Dr. Mariella is involved in the clinical direction and growth of Trauma Healing Therapy. Her work includes:

-   Training and mentoring Trauma Healing Therapy clinicians in trauma-informed care
-   Providing EMDR consultation and supporting clinicians developing advanced EMDR skills
-   Supporting couples therapists in working with relationship trauma, infidelity, attachment injuries, and high-conflict dynamics
-   Developing clinical programs, including trauma intensives, couples therapy services, and specialized offerings
-   Ensuring the practice remains grounded in ethical, evidence-based, culturally thoughtful care
-   Leading community outreach and education around trauma, relationships, emotional safety, and healing
-   Supporting the growth of the clinical team so clients are matched with therapists who can meet their needs with depth and care

Dr. Mariella believes that excellent therapy begins long before a client enters the room. It begins with thoughtful hiring, strong clinical training, ongoing consultation, and a practice culture where therapists are supported in doing deep, meaningful work.
