## What are EMDR Intensives?

EMDR is a well-established and evidence-based therapeutic approach for addressing trauma and PTSD. Traditionally conducted in weekly sessions, EMDR can also be offered in an intensive format, involving more frequent and extended sessions over a shorter period. EMDR intensives are designed to expedite the healing process, allowing clients to experience significant progress in a condensed time frame.

## Why Choose EMDR Intensives?

**1\. Rapid Symptom Reduction**
You might have considered entering into regular therapy sessions for quite some time. However, the constant chaos and demands in your schedule make the idea of a weekly therapy commitment more daunting than supportive. The thought of spending months or years following the traditional weekly therapy model seems overwhelming to you.

We got you covered! EMDR intensives often lead to a more rapid reduction of trauma-related symptoms compared to traditional weekly sessions. This concentrated approach allows clients to delve deeply into the therapeutic process, promoting accelerated healing.

**2\. Flexible Scheduling**
Our EMDR intensives offer flexibility in scheduling, catering to individuals who prefer a more condensed and time-efficient treatment option. This can be especially beneficial for those with busy lifestyles or those seeking an immersive therapeutic experience.

**3\. Personalized Treatment Plans**
At Trauma Healing Therapy, we understand that each individual’s journey is unique. Our experienced EMDR therapists work closely with clients to create personalized treatment plans tailored to their specific needs, ensuring a comprehensive and effective healing experience.


**4\. Expert Therapists**
Our therapists are highly trained and experienced in providing EMDR therapy, including intensives. We prioritize the safety and well-being of our clients and adhere to the highest standards of ethical practice. Rest assured that you are in capable and compassionate hands throughout your healing journey.

**5\. Cost and time effective**
EMDR intensives can be considered cost-effective compared to traditional EMDR weekly therapy for several reasons:

-   **Time Efficiency:** EMDR intensives typically involve more extended sessions conducted over a shorter period of tim. This concentrated approach allows individuals to make significant progress in a shorter time frame compared to the gradual progress in weekly therapy. The reduced overall time commitment may translate to cost savings.
-   **Accelerated Healing:** The concentrated nature of EMDR intensives can lead to accelerated healing. Clients may address and process traumatic experiences more efficiently during intensives, potentially reducing the overall number of sessions needed.
-   **Reduced Total Sessions:** EMDR intensives aim to achieve therapeutic goals within a condensed schedule. This may result in a lower total number of sessions compared to traditional weekly therapy, potentially reducing overall costs.

FAQs on EMDR intensives

How do I know if EMDR Intensives are right for me?

Deciding whether EMDR Intensives are the appropriate choice for you involves a thoughtful evaluation of various factors. The nature and severity of your trauma, your personal preferences, and the assessment conducted by our skilled therapists all play pivotal roles in this determination. At Trauma Healing Therapy, we prioritize your well-being and offer, after the free 20 minute consultation, a comprehensive initial consultation (for a fee) before proceeding with the intensive program, so you won’t have to invest in a full program unless a fit is determined. This consultation serves as a crucial step in understanding your unique needs, allowing us to collaboratively explore whether EMDR intensives align with your therapeutic goals. Our goal is to ensure that the chosen approach resonates with your individual experiences and provides the most effective path towards healing.

Total cost and amount of meetings

EMDR intensives encompass programs spanning from meeting once to several days, featuring an individualized treatment strategy crafted to align with your unique therapeutic goals. Costs are determined based on the specific length of your uniquely tailored program, with clear communication of fees in advance. It is to be noted that EMDR intensives are still very cost effective compared to weekly EMDR therapy sessions, due to the short-term nature of this therapeutic approach.
