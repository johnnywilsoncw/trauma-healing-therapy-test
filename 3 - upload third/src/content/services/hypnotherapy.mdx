Hypnotherapy is a technique that utilizes hypnosis to alleviate specific symptoms or health conditions. Hypnosis is defined as a state of deep relaxation and internally focused attention. Hypnotherapy works by inducing a hypnotic state to help treating symptoms of anxiety and phobias, as well as PTSD symptoms. Hypnotherapy may also been used to help with pain management, weight loss, smoking cessation, etc.
